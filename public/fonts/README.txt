Hongchuang Demo CJK Regular is a TrueType conversion of Noto Sans CJK SC Regular, extracted from system TTC face 2 and subset to GB2312 plus application characters (7573 codepoints) for this synthetic Chinese report demo. CFF outlines were converted to quadratic TrueType outlines with fontTools Cu2QuPen (maximum error 1 unit) to permit valid PDF TrueType embedding. The build-time reduced TTF is embedded intact: runtime fontkit subsetting is disabled because visual verification found missing glyph outlines. The family was renamed Hongchuang Demo CJK. Original SIL Open Font License and attribution are in LICENSE-Noto.txt. No real patient data. Unsupported characters are explicitly rejected by the PDF generator, never silently omitted.
